# R Programming Projects

This repository contains projects completed as part of the R Programming Workshop. Each project focuses on data cleaning, statistical analysis, data visualization, and predictive modelling using R.

## Project List

1. **Stock Price Trend and Volatility Analysis** – Analyse historical stock prices, returns, and moving averages to identify market trends and periods of high volatility.
2. **Comparative Analysis of Selected Stocks** – Compare multiple stocks based on returns, risk, volatility, and correlation to evaluate their relative performance.
3. **Stock Market Crash/Anomaly Detection** – Identify unusual price movements, major drawdowns, and potential market crash periods.
4. **Library Book Circulation and User Behaviour Analysis** – Analyse borrowing patterns across books, genres, user categories, and time periods to identify popular resources.
5. **Library Demand Prediction and Book Recommendation Analysis** – Predict book demand and develop a basic recommendation system using historical borrowing patterns.
6. **Heart Disease Risk Factor Analysis** – Explore relationships between health indicators and heart disease, using classification models to identify risk patterns.
7. **Diabetes Risk and Lifestyle Analysis** – Analyse demographic, lifestyle, and health indicators associated with diabetes and build predictive models.
8. **Indian Liver Disease Data Analysis** – Examine demographic and biochemical indicators to identify patterns associated with liver disease.
9. **Maternal Health Risk Analysis** – Study health indicators to understand maternal risk categories and predict risk levels.
10. **Air Quality and Pollution Analysis** – Analyse pollutant measurements, identify pollution trends, and study relationships between different pollutants.
11. **Climate Change and Global Temperature Analysis** – Examine long-term temperature trends across countries and regions using statistical analysis.
12. **Forest Fires: Weather and Fire Occurrence Analysis** – Investigate the relationship between weather conditions and forest-fire occurrence and severity.
13. **Urban Bike Sharing and Weather Analysis** – Analyse bike rental demand based on weather, seasons, and time periods.
14. **Renewable Energy Production Analysis** – Explore renewable energy generation trends and their relationship with environmental conditions.
15. **Rainfall and Water Resource Analysis** – Study rainfall patterns, seasonal variations, and regional differences.
16. **Wine Quality Analysis** – Explore relationships between physicochemical properties and wine quality ratings.
17. **Student Performance Analysis** – Analyse demographic, social, and academic factors associated with student performance.
18. **Online Retail Customer and Sales Analysis** – Identify sales trends, popular products, customer purchasing patterns, and revenue drivers.
19. **Human Activity Recognition Using Smartphone Data** – Analyse smartphone sensor data to identify and classify different human activities.
20. **Credit Card Default Risk Analysis** – Examine financial and demographic factors associated with credit card defaults and predict default risk.
21. **Air Quality and Pollution Analysis** – Identify pollution trends and unusually high pollutant measurements using threshold-based anomaly detection.
22. **Student Performance Analysis** – Explore factors influencing student grades using statistical analysis and linear regression.
23. **Bank Marketing Campaign Analysis** – Analyse customer responses to bank marketing campaigns and build a model to predict campaign outcomes.

## Tools and Technologies
- **Language:** R
- **Data Manipulation:** dplyr
- **Data Visualization:** ggplot2, Base R
- **Statistical Analysis:** Descriptive Statistics, Correlation, Regression
- **Machine Learning:** Decision Trees, k-NN, Random Forest

## Repository Structure
Projects are organized into separate folders, with datasets, R scripts, visualizations, and documentation wherever applicable.
